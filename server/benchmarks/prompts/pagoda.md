# 3D PAGODA BENCHMARK — SPRING FESTIVAL AT THE FIVE-STORY PAGODA

Build a polished, interactive 3D voxel scene as a single HTML page.

This is a visual and technical benchmark. Every model gets the same brief;
the scene you produce is compared side by side with other models' scenes.

# VOXEL ENGINE

Generate voxels procedurally using:

`BoxGeometry + InstancedMesh`

Do NOT create one Mesh per voxel.

Target:

**15,000–30,000 visible voxels**

with approximately:

**4,000 pagoda voxels**

Group voxels by material/color.

Use convincing voxel AO/depth.

Use `MeshStandardMaterial`.

---

# LIGHTING

Use:

* 1 DirectionalLight
* 1 HemisphereLight
* 4 lantern PointLights
* PCF soft shadows
* 2048 shadow map
* ACESFilmicToneMapping
* pixel ratio capped at 1.5

```js
renderer.toneMapping = THREE.ACESFilmicToneMapping;
renderer.setPixelRatio(Math.min(devicePixelRatio, 1.5));
```

Target 55+ FPS.

---

# INTERACTION

Use `OrbitControls`.

* slow auto-rotate when idle
* clicking a lantern toggles its light
* clicking a pagoda floor opens/closes its doors
* doors animate smoothly

Add a ~2.5 second camera intro from the torii toward the pagoda.

# QUALITY CHECK

Before answering, review your scene and fix any problem:

1. Five pagoda floors are visible.
2. Roofs are curved/stepped rather than flat.
3. The scene is not excessively dark or completely black.
4. No obvious z-fighting.
5. No broken or floating geometry.
6. No missing materials.
7. No obvious clipping.
8. Lighting looks intentional.
9. The pagoda remains the visual focal point.
10. No console errors.
11. `window.__VOXEL__` exists and `pagodaFloors === 5`.

Does it look logically composed? Does the pagoda actually look like a
five-story Japanese pagoda? Does the scene feel like a polished visual
benchmark rather than a basic voxel demo?

You may add features that clearly improve the scene.

The page contains only the pagoda scene and its HUD.
