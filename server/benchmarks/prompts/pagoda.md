# PART 1 — THE FLOATING ISLAND

* Build the main island on a terrain grid of at least **144×128 cells**. The
  solid land must span at least 128×112 cells and at least five times the
  width of the pagoda's stone plinth along both axes. Use the same cell
  scale for the terrain footprint and the plinth measurement.
* Reserve a full side terrace for the bamboo forest, a separate open terrace
  for the Zen garden, and a broad valley where the three pools step down
  toward the cliff. Leave enough ground between the terraces for paths,
  transition planting, and exposed rock.
* Shape an irregular, asymmetrical rim with projecting ledges, recessed
  coves, and at least three traversable terrace elevations. Join the terraces
  with stairs or sloped stone paths and support them with stepped retaining
  walls where needed.
* Give the island a substantial, tapered underside with layered rock strata,
  cliff shelves, and patches of exposed roots and moss. Include two smaller
  floating islands at different heights, each with its own silhouette and
  planted ledges.

# PART 2 — THE FIVE-STORY PAGODA

* Build a ~28 m tall Goju-no-To with five distinct, progressively smaller
  stories. Give each story a stepped jade roof with upturned corners,
  overhanging eaves, and visible timber brackets underneath.
* Add a tiered stone plinth, vermillion pillars, recessed doors, balcony
  railings, gold roof accents, a stacked finial, and warm interior light
  visible through the doorways.
* Detail the sides and rear with the same structural elements so the pagoda
  holds up from any orbit angle.

# PART 3 — THE GARDEN ROUTE

* Use a continuous stone route to link the torii arrival court, cascade
  garden, bamboo forest, Zen garden, and upper temple courtyard. Give
  visitors a path into the forest and a viewing path along the Zen garden's
  edge.
* Start at the torii with stone steps, bamboo fencing, and two jizo statues.
  Take the main path across the koi pond on an arched wooden bridge with
  railings and visible supports, then zig-zag up to the pagoda courtyard.
* Give the bamboo forest a footprint of at least 32×24 cells and at least
  36 tall stalks clustered on both sides of a winding path. Use segmented
  green stems, visible joints, angled leaves, and varying heights. Leave a
  break in the canopy where the path enters so its route reads from above.
* Give the Zen garden a gravel bed of at least 28×20 cells edged in stone.
  Model visible parallel rake lines and concentric arcs around three
  asymmetrical rock groupings with moss at their bases. Keep a clear open
  view of the gravel from the main camera.
* Place four glowing lanterns at landmarks along the route. Use varied paving
  stones, mossy joints, and planted borders to distinguish the areas.
* Plant three sakura, two pine, two maple, and one willow, for eight
  ornamental trees in addition to the bamboo. Give each species a distinct
  branching and canopy shape. Distribute 40 flowers in clusters beside
  paths, under trees, and along the water's edge.

# PART 4 — WATER AND WILDLIFE

* Build **three separate pools at three different elevations**. Give each a
  clearly visible water surface and its own shoreline. Use a rocky spring
  basin at the top, a broad middle koi pond with six animated koi and a
  planted bank, and a shallow lower pool edged with reeds and stepping
  stones. Give each pool at least 12×10 cells of open water.
* Connect the pools with short streams and stepped cascades. Send the lower
  pool's outlet over the cliff as the main waterfall. Show white water at
  the drops, koi ripples in the middle pond, and mist under the waterfall.
* Animate flying birds around the island and falling sakura petals around
  the flowering trees. Keep their paths clear of solid geometry.

# VOXEL ENGINE

Generate voxels procedurally using:

`BoxGeometry + InstancedMesh`

Do NOT create one Mesh per voxel.

Target:

**40,000–65,000 visible voxels**

with approximately:

**4,000 pagoda voxels**

Group voxels by material/color.

Build the terrain from its visible surface and cliff faces. Spend voxel
detail on paths, roof edges, banks, and rock strata.

Use convincing voxel AO/depth.

Use `MeshStandardMaterial`.

```js
renderer.setPixelRatio(Math.min(devicePixelRatio, 1.5));
```

Target 55+ FPS.
