# THE LOOK

A lush Japanese temple garden on a floating island, drifting above an
endless **sea of clouds** in a quiet valley of towering stone pillars.

Picture the opening frame: a pale, hazy sky; soft white cloud banks
filling the whole lower half of the screen to the horizon; tall green-
capped karst pillars rising out of the clouds on every side, fading into
blue haze with distance; and in the middle, the island, overflowing with
trees, flowers and water, with a tall slender five-story pagoda rising
from its highest point against the sky. Small rock-pillar islets with
bonsai pines float around it. White cranes circle. Waterfalls pour off the
rim and vanish into the clouds below.

Style:

**Premium high-density voxel diorama × ukiyo-e landscape × Chinese
mountain-and-mist painting.** Small voxels and a lot of them. Abundant,
overgrown, colorful, and soft: vegetation spills over every edge, light is
gentle and atmospheric, and nothing reads as a flat grid or an empty plane.

# PART 1 — THE SKY VALLEY

* A **cloud sea** fills the world below the island out to the horizon:
  layered, rounded banks of soft white voxels (or instanced soft puffs)
  with brighter tops and cooler grey-blue shadowed undersides, slowly
  drifting. Lighter wisps of cloud weave between the background pillars.
* At least **10 karst stone pillars** of different heights and widths stand
  in a loose ring at middle and far distance, rising straight out of the
  clouds. Give them stepped vertical rock faces and green caps with a few
  trees. Distant pillars become paler and bluer through fog that matches
  the sky color, so the scene has real aerial depth.
* Use `THREE.Fog` or `FogExp2` tuned to the sky so far geometry dissolves
  into haze rather than ending at a hard edge. Use a vertical sky gradient.
* Keep all cloud geometry outside the camera's reachable orbit range: the
  camera must never end up inside a cloud.

# PART 2 — THE ISLAND

* A rounded, irregular island roughly **160×130 voxels** across, rising
  toward a high terrace for the pagoda, with at least three traversable
  levels joined by stone stairs. On one side, a tall mossy rock crag
  climbs above the garden with pines clinging to it and a waterfall
  springing from it.
* A deep, tapered underside of layered rock strata in warm browns and
  greys, with ledges, hanging roots, and long curtains of vines and moss
  that drape over the rim and down the cliffs.
* The rim is never bare: trees, shrubs, flowers and vines overflow it.
* **6–8 small satellite islets** float around the island at different
  heights: narrow rock pillars with flat grassy tops, each crowned with a
  bonsai-style pine, maple or sakura. A **floating staircase of stone
  slabs** descends from the torii gate down and away from the island.

# PART 3 — THE PAGODA AND TEMPLE

* A tall, slender **five-story pagoda** (Goju-no-To), the tallest object
  on the island and its clear focal point, at least **70 voxels tall**
  including its spire. Five progressively smaller stories with vermillion
  walls and pillars, white or gold trim, and deep **jade-teal roofs with
  layered, upturned eaves** and visible brackets beneath each roof.
* Balconies with railings, recessed doors, warm interior light at night,
  and a tall **gold sorin spire** with stacked rings on top.
* Detail all four sides so it holds up from any orbit angle.
* Below it, a temple hall with a curved jade roof, a large vermillion
  **torii gate** at the arrival point, stone lanterns, and a courtyard.

# PART 4 — THE GARDEN

* A red **arched wooden bridge** with railings over a koi pond. Six
  animated koi, ripples, lily pads and reeds.
* At least **two waterfalls** that pour off the island's rim and fall
  into the clouds, with white water and mist at the base. Water flows
  from the crag spring through streams and the pond to the rim.
* Dense planting with varied, distinct tree species: sakura, maples, pines,
  a weeping willow, rounded shrubs, a small bamboo grove, and clusters of
  colorful flowers (pink, purple, orange, yellow, white) along every path
  and bank. Trees have trunks, branches, and layered, lumpy canopies, not
  single cubes or spheres.
* A small raked Zen gravel garden with rock groupings, and a winding stone
  path linking torii, bridge, temple, and pagoda terrace.
* At least six glowing lanterns along the path (stone tōrō and hanging
  red paper lanterns).

# SEASONS

A season selector with **Spring / Summer / Autumn / Winter**, crossfading
the whole island over about one second:

* **Spring:** sakura in full pink bloom, fresh light greens, falling petals.
* **Summer:** deep saturated greens everywhere, sakura in leaf.
* **Autumn:** maples and most broadleaf trees in red, orange, and gold, with
  falling leaves; pines stay green.
* **Winter:** snow on roofs, ground, rocks, and branch tops; bare deciduous
  branches; drifting snowflakes; frozen-looking pond.

Recolor instances (e.g. `setColorAt`) rather than rebuilding the scene.

# VOXEL ENGINE

Generate voxels procedurally using:

`BoxGeometry + InstancedMesh`

Do NOT create one Mesh per voxel.

Target:

**120,000–250,000 visible voxels**

with approximately:

**8,000 pagoda voxels**

Skip voxels that are fully enclosed on all six sides. Group voxels by
material. Vary colors per voxel with subtle noise so surfaces don't look
flat. Use convincing voxel AO (darken voxels by occlusion from neighbors).

Use `MeshStandardMaterial`.

---

# LIGHTING

Use:

* 1 DirectionalLight (sun/moon) with PCF soft shadows, 2048 shadow map,
  shadow camera fitted tightly to the island
* 1 HemisphereLight (sky blue over warm ground)
* up to 6 lantern PointLights
* ACESFilmicToneMapping, sRGB output
* pixel ratio capped at 1.5

```js
renderer.toneMapping = THREE.ACESFilmicToneMapping;
renderer.setPixelRatio(Math.min(devicePixelRatio, 1.5));
```

Daytime should be bright, soft and airy, never dark or muddy.

Target 55+ FPS.

# TIME OF DAY

Time presets **Dawn / Day / Sunset / Night** that smoothly change the sun,
sky gradient, fog color, and cloud tint:

* **Dawn:** pink-peach sky, golden low light, mist.
* **Day:** pale blue sky, bright white clouds.
* **Sunset:** orange-violet sky, long warm light, glowing cloud tops.
* **Night:** deep blue sky with stars and a moon, clouds lit in cool blue,
  lanterns and pagoda windows glowing warm.

Also let time run as a continuous 90-second day/night cycle that can be
paused. Lanterns come on automatically at dusk and flicker.

# INTERACTION

Use `OrbitControls`: drag to look around, scroll to zoom, right-drag to
move. Slow auto-rotate when idle.

* clicking a lantern toggles its light
* clicking a pagoda floor opens/closes its doors, animating smoothly

**Camera views:** a dropdown with at least **Overview, Torii, Bridge,
Pagoda, Waterfall** that smoothly flies the camera to each view.

Add a ~2.5 second camera intro that sweeps in from the cloud sea and ends
on the Overview: an elevated three-quarter view with the whole island
centered and filling about 60% of the viewport width, cloud sea across the
lower half of the frame, karst pillars on both sides and behind, satellite
islets around the island, and the pagoda silhouetted against the sky.

Limit orbiting so the camera stays above the cloud tops while still
allowing a low angle that shows the island's underside and waterfalls.

# HUD

Minimal and elegant, never covering the island:

* Top left: the title `Garden Above the Clouds` in a large white serif
  (system serif stack, soft shadow), with a small hint line below:
  `Drag to look around, scroll to zoom, right-drag to move`.
* Bottom center: one row of translucent frosted-glass pill controls:
  camera view dropdown | `Spring` `Summer` `Autumn` `Winter` |
  `Dawn` `Day` `Sunset` `Night` | `More` menu. The active option is
  highlighted.
* `More` opens a small panel with: time-of-day slider, pause cycle,
  auto-rotate toggle, and a small stats readout (`48k voxels | 60 FPS`).

Calculate voxel count and FPS dynamically.

Expose:

```js
window.__VOXEL__ = {
  voxelCount,
  fps,
  pagodaFloors,
  timeOfDay,
  season
};
```

`pagodaFloors` must equal `5`.

# QUALITY CHECK

Before answering, review your scene and fix any problem:

1. The opening view reads instantly as a floating garden island over a
   sea of clouds, with karst pillars fading into haze around it.
2. Five pagoda floors are visible; the pagoda is tall, slender, and the
   focal point, with jade-teal upturned roofs and a gold spire.
3. The island looks lush and overflowing: no bare flat ground, no empty
   rim, vines and plants spill over the edges.
4. At least two waterfalls fall off the rim into the clouds.
5. Satellite islets and the floating stone stairs are visible.
6. All four seasons and all four time presets visibly transform the scene.
7. The scene is never excessively dark or completely black.
8. No z-fighting, floating or broken geometry, or obvious clipping.
9. The camera can never enter a cloud.
10. No console errors.
11. `window.__VOXEL__` exists and `pagodaFloors === 5`.
12. Orbit to the rear and sides to verify that architecture, planting, and
    cliff detail hold up from all angles.

Does it look like a premium voxel art piece, the kind of image people
share? Does the pagoda look like a real five-story Japanese pagoda?

You may add features that clearly improve the scene.
