05 / Before the first pour

A few beans of wisdom.

Choosing a roast, setting a schedule, and knowing what happens next—answered before the kettle clicks on.

How do I choose a roast?

Start with the flavor you want most. Morning Light is a medium roast with chocolate and caramel notes, Daybreak is a light roast with citrus and honey notes, and Nightcap Decaf is a decaf medium roast with cocoa and hazelnut notes. You can change the featured roast before confirming your demo subscription.

Which grind should I choose?

Choose whole bean if you grind immediately before brewing, filter grind for filter coffee, or espresso grind for an espresso machine. In the fictional service, you can request one of these three grind choices with your delivery.

Can I pause or change a delivery?

In this fictional service, customers can pause or change their next delivery. This offline benchmark demonstrates the selection and confirmation experience only; it does not create, prepare, or schedule an actual delivery.

How often will the coffee arrive?

You can choose every 2 weeks or monthly. The per-delivery price stays the same for either schedule, and billing follows the selected delivery schedule.

How does this demo work?

This delivered benchmark is an offline demo and does not connect to the fictional Ember & Bean shop. Your selections update the coffee bag, order summary, and local confirmation, but no order is placed, no payment is taken, and no information is sent.
