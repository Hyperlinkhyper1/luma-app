How the signalling works

Each coloured railway section is an electronic block. It accepts only one train. A signal is green for two or more clear sections, yellow for one clear section, and red when the next section is occupied, held by an operator, or its point route is unavailable.

Points move before their route locks. Conflicting point paths are exclusive. Trains brake to the red board and may proceed only after the interlocking proves their route clear.

The passing loop at North Vale is single track in both directions. Its direction lock admits one train at a time; another waits in the loop. Click any signal to place a manual hold.
