How it works

Scale without popping: a logarithmic depth buffer keeps the meter-scale hull and the 100,000-light-year star field in one continuous camera pass. The galaxy uses bounded 1,000-light-year scene units, while local systems use a separate visual scale layer.

Procedural galaxy: 220,000 GPU point stars use logarithmic arm coordinates, temperature-based color, arm dust attenuation, differential angular velocity, a central bar, bulge, halo, and a shader-based lensing pass.

Route interaction: switch to Galaxy map, then click the main star chart or minimap. Aurora will build a new three-dimensional course and fly it automatically.
