HOW IT WORKS
One unit represents roughly one light-year in the galactic coordinate layer, while the ship and planets render in metres inside scaled system groups. Dynamic camera-relative placement, logarithmic depth, frustum-culled instancing and controlled point sizes preserve detail without visible precision popping. The black-hole ring bends the final composited image near the core.
