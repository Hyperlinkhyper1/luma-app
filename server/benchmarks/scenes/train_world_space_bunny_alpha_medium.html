How the signalling works

Block occupancy: every car is tracked independently. A section is red on the diagram whenever any part of any train is standing on it.

  • Green: this section and the following section are clear.
  • Yellow: this section is clear, but the one after it is occupied. A train slows and prepares to stop.
  • Red: the section is occupied, has no available locked route, or a person has placed a manual hold.

A train may only enter the next section after its signal clears. Turnout blades move during route setting, then remain locked while that section is occupied. The central stone bridge is an exclusive, direction-locked single-track section; conflicting reservations are rejected.

Routes are released as soon as the last car clears. Passenger trains dwell at platforms while their entry signal remains red, allowing following movements to use the passing sections.
