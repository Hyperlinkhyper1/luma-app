Meet the morning shelf

Three roasts. Three ways to wake.

Each coffee is roasted in small, thoughtful batches and sent while its flavors are still singing.

01 / Easygoing

Morning Light

Medium roast · 250g

Round and grounding, with dark chocolate, burnt caramel, and a soft flash of toasted almond. The dependable cup that makes an early start feel unhurried.

02 / Bright-eyed

Daybreak

Light roast · 250g

Lifted and sunlit: Meyer lemon brightness meets wildflower honey. Lovely as filter coffee, bright enough to wake the whole room.

03 / After hours

Nightcap Decaf

Decaf medium roast · 250g

Deep cocoa, hazelnut, and a velvety finish, crafted for late evenings and decaf drinkers who still want the whole ritual.
