FAQ

How fresh is the coffee?

Each bag is roasted to order and shipped within 48 hours of roasting, ensuring peak freshness for your brewing ritual.

Can I choose my grind?

Yes. You can select whole bean, filter grind, or espresso grind when you set up your subscription.

Can I pause or skip a delivery?

In the fictional service you can pause or change your next delivery at any time through your account.

How does this demo work?

This delivered benchmark is an offline demo and does not connect to the fictional Ember & Bean shop. No orders are placed, no payments are taken, and no information is sent.
