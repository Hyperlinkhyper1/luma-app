Frequently asked questions

What roasts are available?

We offer three roasts: Morning Light (medium, chocolate & caramel), Daybreak (light, citrus & honey), and Nightcap Decaf (medium decaf, cocoa & hazelnut).

What grind options do you provide?

Whole bean, medium‑fine filter grind, and fine espresso grind are available for each roast.

How can I pause or change my next delivery?

Log into your account (in the fictional service) to pause, skip, or adjust the number of bags and delivery frequency at any time before the next shipment.
