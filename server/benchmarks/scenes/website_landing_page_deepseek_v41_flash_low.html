Roasted with care

Every batch is roasted in small quantities and listened to — the crack of the beans tells the roaster when to pull the drum. We taste each roast before it's allowed into a bag, and we rest it a day so it can breathe before shipping.

FAQ

Questions before the kettle boils.

How do I choose between the three roasts?

Follow your mornings. Morning Light is a medium roast with chocolate and caramel notes — round and forgiving, good with milk. Daybreak is a light roast with citrus and honey notes, best when you want something bright and filter-brewed. Nightcap Decaf is a medium decaf roast with cocoa and hazelnut notes for the cup after dinner.

You can switch roasts at any delivery, and you can taste them all by choosing Daily duo.

Can I get my coffee ground for my brewer?

Yes. Every delivery lets you pick whole bean, filter grind, or espresso grind. Whole bean keeps the aroma longest if you have a grinder at home; filter grind suits pour-over and drip; espresso grind is milled fine for a basket and a firm tamp.

Can I pause or change a delivery?

Yes. In the fictional Ember & Bean service you can pause a delivery, skip the next one, change your grind, or swap your roast before the bag is roasted. The idea is a rhythm that fits your shelf, not a schedule that fills it up.

What does “per delivery” mean for billing?

Each price is charged per delivery, not per month. Solo ritual is $14 per delivery, Daily duo is $26 per delivery, and Coffee household is $48 per delivery — the same whichever frequency you choose.

Billing follows the selected delivery schedule: choose Every 2 weeks and you're charged each time a delivery goes out; choose Monthly and you're charged once a month. Shipping is included in this demo pricing.
