How the signalling works

Each numbered block may contain only one train. A signal is red when its next block is occupied, its route is unavailable, or an operator has held it. It is yellow when one clear block leads to red; it is green only when two clear blocks are available.

The bridge is a single-track resource. Its entry route is reserved in one direction, switch blades move, the route locks, and it releases only after the entire train has cleared. Trains brake to the red and wait. Stations reverse locomotives on a loop, so cars always remain physically on the same continuous rails.
