FAQ

Common questions about the roasts and the demo.

How do I choose a roast?

Think about the cup you want. Morning Light is a medium roast with chocolate and caramel notes—balanced and comforting. Daybreak is light, bright with citrus and honey. Nightcap Decaf is a decaf medium roast with cocoa and hazelnut. Decaf is not caffeine-free.

What grind should I choose?

Whole bean if you grind at home. Filter grind for pour-over and drip brewers. Espresso grind for espresso machines and moka pots. You can change it with your next delivery.

Can I pause or change deliveries?

Yes. In this fictional service you can pause or change your next delivery at any time before it ships. Delivery follows the schedule you select: every two weeks or monthly.

How does this demo work?

This is an offline benchmark demo. It does not connect to a real Ember & Bean shop, no order is placed, no payment is taken, and no information is sent anywhere.
