FAQ

How do I choose a roast?
You can select a roast in the hero section, on each roast card, or in the coffee selection dialog. Your choice is remembered and used for the next delivery unless you change it.
What grind options are available?
We offer whole bean (for you to grind), filter grind (for drip/pour‑over), and espresso grind (for espresso machines). Choose your preferred grind in the selection dialog.
Can I pause or change my next delivery?
Yes. In the fictional service you can skip, pause, or adjust the grind, roast, or frequency of any upcoming delivery from your account.
How does this demo work?
This is an offline benchmark. No real orders are placed, no payments are processed, and no data is sent elsewhere. All interactions are simulated locally to showcase the UI and flows.
