How Signalling Works

  • The track is divided into BLOCKS; each holds at most one train.
  • Signals show RED (stop), YELLOW (next block occupied), GREEN (safe).
  • Before a switch clears, the ROUTE is reserved and blades locked.
  • The bridge/tunnel section is single track: trains wait in the passing loop.
  • Click signals or the diagram to hold them red manually.
