FAQ

Questions about roasts, grind, deliveries, and how this demo works.

Which roast should I choose?

Start with Morning Light if you want chocolate and caramel in a balanced medium cup. Daybreak is lighter, with citrus and honey. Nightcap Decaf is a decaf medium roast with cocoa and hazelnut—gentle on evenings, though decaf still means trace caffeine, not none.

What grind options are available?

Whole bean if you grind at home, filter grind for pour-over and drip, or espresso grind for your machine. You can change your grind before any delivery.

Can I pause or change my deliveries?

Yes. Pause or shift your next delivery whenever you like. Billing follows your selected delivery schedule, and prices stay the same per delivery regardless of frequency.

How does this demo work?

This page is an offline benchmark demo. It does not connect to a real Ember & Bean shop, takes no payments, collects no address details, and sends no information anywhere. Submitting the form shows a local confirmation only.

What do the prices include?

Prices shown are per delivery: $14 for Solo ritual, $26 for Daily duo, and $48 for Coffee household. Shipping is included in this demo pricing, and the total does not change between every 2 weeks and monthly delivery.
