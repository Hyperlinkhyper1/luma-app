HOW SIGNALLING WORKS
The line is divided into blocks; each block holds at most one train.
Red: block ahead occupied or route not set.
Yellow: block clear, but the signal after it is red.
Green: at least two blocks ahead are clear.
Before passing switches, a route is reserved and the switches lock until released.
The single-track section (bridge→tunnel) is direction-locked; trains wait in the passing loop.
Conflicting routes can never be reserved at the same time, so the system cannot deadlock.
