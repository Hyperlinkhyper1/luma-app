Our coffee

Three roasts, one slow morning

Every 250g bag is roasted in small batches, rested just long enough to degas, and shipped at its aromatic peak. Pick the cup that matches your ritual.

Roasted with care

Drum-roasted in 12kg batches, logged by hand, and rested 48 hours before it ever meets a mailbag. No roast leaves the drum tired.

Made for your brew

Whole bean for the grinders, filter grind for the pour-over, espresso grind for the portafilter. Tell us your kettle and we'll match the grind.

Fresh coffee, on your schedule

Every two weeks or monthly — your rhythm, your rules. Pause before a holiday week or swap roasts between deliveries, all in the service.
