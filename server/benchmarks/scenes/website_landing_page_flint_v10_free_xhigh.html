The lineup

Our coffee

Three roasts, one point of view: coffee should taste like the morning, not like a factory. Each batch is small, roasted with intention, and tuned for the way you brew.

Morning Light

Morning Light

Medium roast with chocolate and caramel notes.

A rounded medium roast for milk drinks, drip, and quiet mornings that feel warm rather than bright.

Daybreak

Daybreak

Light roast with citrus and honey notes.

A lighter roast that keeps the morning awake with sparkle before softening into sweetness.

Nightcap Decaf

Nightcap Decaf

Decaf medium roast with cocoa and hazelnut notes.

A calm, cozy decaf with cocoa and hazelnut. It is a medium roast and contains caffeine.

Roasted with care

Beans are small-batch roasted to order, then cooled and rested for a clean, vivid cup. The roast is timed like a kitchen experiment, not a factory line.

Made for your brew

Roast and grind guidance helps you match Morning Light, Daybreak, or Nightcap Decaf to whole bean, filter grind, or espresso grind.
