COMPRESSED SCALE LAYERS
The vessel and planetary system are modeled at local visual scale. The galaxy is a compressed astronomical layer, with one scene unit corresponding to roughly 2,200 light-years in the HUD.

SEAMLESS TRANSITIONS
System geometry crossfades while the ship climbs into the galactic layer. Logarithmic depth, bounded point sizes, and distance-scaled shaders keep tiny and immense objects stable.

CORE OPTICS
A screen-space lens pass bends the rendered star field around the projected event horizon. Star rotation is differential, while the dust and H-II layers are composited independently.
