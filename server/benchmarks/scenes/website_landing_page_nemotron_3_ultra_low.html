FAQ

How do I choose the right roast?

Morning Light is a balanced medium roast with chocolate and caramel — great for most brew methods. Daybreak is a bright light roast with citrus and honey, ideal for pour‑over or AeroPress. Nightcap Decaf is a medium roast with cocoa and hazelnut, perfect for evening espresso or cold brew. You can switch roasts on any upcoming delivery.

What grind options are available?

We offer whole bean, filter grind (suitable for V60, Chemex, drip), and espresso grind (fine for portafilter machines). The grind is selected per delivery and can be changed before the next shipment.

Can I pause or change my next delivery?

Yes. In the fictional Ember & Bean account you can pause a delivery, swap the roast, adjust the grind, or modify the plan up until the shipping cutoff. Changes apply to the following delivery.

How does this demo work?

This page is an offline benchmark — no real orders are placed, no payment is taken, and no data is sent anywhere. The subscription dialog simulates a checkout flow and shows a local confirmation with your selections.
