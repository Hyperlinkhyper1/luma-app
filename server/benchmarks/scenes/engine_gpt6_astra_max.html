ATLAS / RENDER PIPELINE

A different kind of horsepower.

Rendering complexity and engine speed are independent. Keep the mechanism slow, even at maximum quality.

Native pixel ratio is capped at 1.5. The drawing buffer is limited to 8 megapixels and 4096 pixels per side. No motion blur or depth-of-field blur is applied.
