Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose my roast?

Use the roast picker in the hero or the “Choose this roast” button on each card to start your order.

What grind options do you offer?

You can pick whole bean, filter grind, or espresso grind in the order dialog.

Can I pause or change my next delivery?

While this is a demo, the dialog explains you can pause or update your next delivery schedule.

Does this demo place a real order?

No! This is an offline demo – no payment, no data sent, just interaction.
