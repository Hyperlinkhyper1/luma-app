3D Fluid Simulation Benchmark - Help

Simulation Controls

Scenario Selection: Choose from 5 scenarios using the buttons or the dropdown.
• Dam Break: Column of water collapses
• Double Dam: Two columns collide
• Drop: Sphere falls into shallow pool
• Faucet: Continuous stream from above
• Wave Machine: Moving wall creates waves

Physics Controls

Particle Count: Adjust number of simulation particles (5,000 - 100,000)
Time Step: Controls simulation accuracy and stability
Viscosity: Resistance to flow (0 = ideal fluid)
Gravity: Strength of gravitational force
Density Error: Incompressibility measure (%)

Camera Controls

Orbit: Left click + drag
Pan: Right click + drag
Zoom: Mouse wheel or right click + drag up/down
Tilt: Use arrow keys or sliders for tank orientation

Interaction

Stir: Click and drag in the water
Tilt Tank: Press arrow keys or use tilt sliders
Drop Object: Press Space to spawn a ball
Reset: Press R or use reset button
Pause: Press P or use pause button
Single Step: Press Shift + P or use step button
Help: Press H

Rendering Options

Screen-Space Rendering: Advanced fluid rendering with refraction and reflection
Raw Particles: See particles as colored dots
Wireframe: View particle structure
Absorption: Water color intensity
Reflection: Mirror-like surface effect
Fresnel: Edge highlight strength

Performance

• Targets 60 FPS on mid-range laptops
• Uses GPU acceleration when available
• Falls back to CPU simulation if WebGL unavailable
• Real-time incompressible fluid simulation
• Advanced screen-space rendering pipeline
