FAQ

What roast can I choose?
You can choose from our three signature roasts: Morning Light (Medium), Daybreak (Light), and Nightcap Decaf (Medium Decaf).
What grind options are available?
We offer three options: Whole Bean, Filter Grind (for drip or pour-over), and Espresso Grind (fine).
Can I pause my deliveries?
Absolutely. You can pause, skip, or change your next delivery at any time through your account dashboard.
How does this demo work?
This is an offline frontend benchmark. No orders are actually placed, no payments are taken, and no information is sent to any server.
