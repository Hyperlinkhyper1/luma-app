HOW IT WORKS

One continuous coordinate field. Galaxy distances use a compressed light-year scale, while hulls and planets use an intentionally enlarged local scale.

logarithmic depth prevents depth conflicts; a bounded far plane and the ship-following camera prevent precision jitter. The 230,000-star galaxy is one GPU point draw. Differential arm rotation happens in its vertex shader.

The black hole’s lensing is a screen-space approximation layered before bloom. The nebula combines a ray-marched volume with procedural sprite layers.
