    FAQ

    Which roast should I choose?

    Our roasts range from light to medium. Choose based on your preference for acidity, sweetness, and body.

    What grind options are available?

    We offer whole bean, filter grind, and espresso grind.

    Can I pause or skip a delivery?

    Yes, you can pause or skip deliveries in your account settings.

    How does this demo work?

    This is an offline demo and does not connect to the Ember & Bean shop. No information is sent or collected.
