SCALE ARCHITECTURE
240,000 stars are evaluated in one GPU particle draw, with radii compressed logarithmically and the galaxy rendered in its own coordinate space. The camera uses a floating-origin rig: local ship and system objects stay at human scale while the distant starfield is rebased around the observer. Logarithmic depth separates 10 m plating from 100,000 light-years without z-fighting. Differential rotation advances orbital phase by radius; shader-space dust, colour, and point sizing keep the disc luminous. The central Einstein ring is a screen-space radial approximation, not physical ray tracing. A single generated scene supplies the departure star, planets, ringed destination, moon, volumetric nebula and original ship.
