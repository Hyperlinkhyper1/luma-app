How the signalling works

Every section of track is a block. A block may contain only one train. Before a train enters the next block, the interlocking reserves the route and locks any switch points. Signals show the state of the route ahead: red means stop, yellow means proceed with caution, and green means two blocks are clear. The single-track tunnel section has a directional lock, so only one direction may reserve it at a time. Click a signal to hold it at red; click it again to release it.
