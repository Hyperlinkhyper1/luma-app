# SCENE

Create a procedural voxel Japanese garden on a large floating island. Set the
torii and bridge in the foreground, the pagoda on a raised rear terrace, and
the waterfall on a side visible from the main overview. The pagoda must
remain the tallest feature and the visual focal point.

# PART 1 — THE FLOATING ISLAND

* Build the main island on a terrain grid of at least **112×96 cells**. The
  land itself must span at least 100×84 cells, measured across its widest
  points. Use the extra space to separate landmarks and give each its own
  surroundings.
* Shape an irregular, asymmetrical rim with projecting ledges, recessed
  coves, and at least three traversable terrace elevations. Join the terraces
  with stairs or sloped stone paths and support them with stepped retaining
  walls where needed.
* Give the island a substantial, tapered underside with layered rock strata,
  cliff shelves, and patches of exposed roots and moss. Include two smaller
  floating islands at different heights, each with its own silhouette and
  planted ledges.

# PART 2 — THE FIVE-STORY PAGODA

* Build a ~28 m tall Goju-no-To with five distinct, progressively smaller
  stories. Give each story a stepped jade roof with upturned corners,
  overhanging eaves, and visible timber brackets underneath.
* Add a tiered stone plinth, vermillion pillars, recessed doors, balcony
  railings, gold roof accents, a stacked finial, and warm interior light
  visible through the doorways.
* Detail the sides and rear with the same structural elements so the pagoda
  holds up from any orbit angle.

# PART 3 — THE GARDEN ROUTE

* Connect four distinct areas with a continuous stone route: a torii arrival
  court, a koi garden, an upper temple courtyard, and a quiet cliffside
  grove reached by a branch from the courtyard.
* Start at the torii with stone steps, bamboo fencing, and two jizo statues.
  Take the main path across the koi pond on an arched wooden bridge with
  railings and visible supports, then zig-zag up to the pagoda courtyard.
* Place four glowing lanterns at landmarks along the route. Use varied paving
  stones, mossy joints, and planted borders to distinguish the areas.
* Place exactly eight trees across the scene: three sakura, two pine, two
  maple, and one willow. Give each species a distinct branching and canopy
  shape. Distribute 40 flowers in clusters beside paths, under trees, and
  along the water's edge. Detail the rear and side terraces as well.

# PART 4 — WATER AND WILDLIFE

* Shape the pond with a shallow planted bank, stepping stones near the edge,
  a deeper pool with six animated koi, and ripples that follow their motion.
* Connect the pond to a visible outlet stream that passes over a rocky lip and
  becomes a waterfall. Show a continuous flow with foam at the lip, mist
  below, and wet rocks along the stream and cliff.
* Animate flying birds around the island and falling sakura petals around
  the flowering trees. Keep their paths clear of solid geometry.

# VOXEL ENGINE

Generate voxels procedurally using:

`BoxGeometry + InstancedMesh`

Do NOT create one Mesh per voxel.

Target:

**28,000–50,000 visible voxels**

with approximately:

**4,000 pagoda voxels**

Group voxels by material/color.

Build the terrain from its visible surface and cliff faces. Spend voxel
detail on paths, roof edges, banks, and rock strata.

Use convincing voxel AO/depth.

Use `MeshStandardMaterial`.

---

# LIGHTING

Use:

* 1 DirectionalLight
* 1 HemisphereLight
* 4 lantern PointLights
* PCF soft shadows
* 2048 shadow map
* ACESFilmicToneMapping
* pixel ratio capped at 1.5

```js
renderer.toneMapping = THREE.ACESFilmicToneMapping;
renderer.setPixelRatio(Math.min(devicePixelRatio, 1.5));
```

Target 55+ FPS.

# INTERACTION

Use `OrbitControls`.

* slow auto-rotate when idle
* clicking a lantern toggles its light
* clicking a pagoda floor opens/closes its doors
* doors animate smoothly

Add a ~2.5 second camera intro from the torii toward the pagoda.
End on a three-quarter view that shows the whole main island, the waterfall,
and the pagoda's five tiers. Allow zooming out to see both smaller islands
and orbiting low enough to inspect the underside.
